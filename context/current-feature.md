## Goals
<!-- Goals and requirements -->

- `Screen — Job Posts`: list panel (search, sort by Fit %/Date added/Company, fit-score badges) + detail panel (company/role header, Edit + Run comparison actions, seniority/location/employment/salary meta tiles, Job Description block, Parsed Requirements panel).
- Parsed Requirements panel: split into **Confirmed** (Must-have/Nice-to-have chip groups; clicking a chip reveals inline controls — required-level dropdown, Must-have/Nice-to-have toggle, remove) and **Unresolved** (phrases the parser couldn't map to the taxonomy, each with "Assign to skill" and "Dismiss" actions that never silently count toward fit score).
- Empty variant of the whole Parsed Requirements panel when parsing finds nothing: "Couldn't detect requirements — add them manually" + manual-add control.
- "Add Job Post" modal (title, company, paste-in description textarea) reached from the list header's "+" button; submitting adds the post and selects it.
- Zero-state for the whole screen when the account has no job posts yet: "No job posts yet — paste your first one" + primary CTA.
- Delete a job post → confirmation dialog warning that its comparisons and any CVs tailored to it are affected too.
